Tips

  • Auto picks the closest string for you. Locked holds the string you tapped, handy for a badly detuned string. Chromatic names any note.
  • Tune up to the note from slightly below. It holds pitch better than coming down from above.
  • Phone speakers barely play anything under 150 Hz. Use headphones or hold the phone near the guitar for the low strings.
